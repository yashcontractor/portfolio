About

A little bit of everything.

They say a jack of all trades is a master of none, but I've never really believed you have to choose just one thing. At 26, I'm working a 9–5 while exploring everything that genuinely interests me: creating content, filmmaking, fitness, technology, travel, and the countless little things that make life interesting. I'm learning, experimenting, and figuring out how to turn those interests into something of my own.

This portfolio is a collection of that process, the content I create, the projects I'm working on, the brands I'm building, and the skills I'm picking up along the way. I don't have a perfectly defined path, and I'm okay with that. I'd rather explore what I'm capable of and build a life around the things I care about than limit myself to one box.
